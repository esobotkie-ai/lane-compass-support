Lane Compass

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Lane Compass is a bowling alignment app made by Earl Sobotkiewicz ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

The short version

  • Your bowler profiles, saved lines and notes stay on your device. We never see them.
  • We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking of any kind.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We use a service called RevenueCat only to confirm your free trial and unlock.

Information stored on your device

Profiles (name, bowling hand, drift, approach length), saved lines (boards, drift, notes, bowling center, lane, oil pattern) and your settings are kept in the app's own storage on your device. They are not sent to us or anyone else.

If you choose to share a line, the picture or PDF you create is sent only to the person or app you pick in the iOS share sheet.

Deleting the app deletes this information from your device.

Purchases (free trial and unlock)

Payments are processed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. We never receive your name, Apple ID, email address or payment details.

To check whether you have started the free trial or unlocked the app, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random identifier created by the app (not linked to your name or Apple ID);
  • your purchase history for Lane Compass;
  • basic technical information needed to process it, such as app version, device type and country.

This is used only to provide the trial, the unlock and Restore Purchases. RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Children

Lane Compass is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
