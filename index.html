Common questions

How are start, laydown and slide worked out?

Take your arrows board minus your pins board and divide by 3. Add that to the arrows board: that's your laydown board. Your slide board is the laydown board plus 5, and your start board is the slide board minus your drift.

Example: arrows 10, pins 7, drift 5. (10 − 7) ÷ 3 = 1, so laydown 11, slide 16, start 11.

What is drift?

How many boards you move sideways during your approach, from where you start to where you slide. Your profile holds your usual drift, and you can change it for any line.

I got a new phone. How do I get my unlock back?

Open Lane Compass, go to the Profiles tab and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought it with.
